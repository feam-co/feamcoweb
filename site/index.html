Architecture Philosophy

Compliance by architecture,
not by assertion.

Four engineering decisions that make FEAM's governance verifiable — not just claimed.

01 ——

⚙ Deterministic Over Probabilistic

Governance decisions never depend on LLM outputs. Our rule engine is explicit, auditable and reproducible. Same input, same output, every time. An LLM that changes its mind cannot change a governance outcome.

Rule Engine
02 ——

🔐 Cryptographic Truth

Every decision is sealed in a SHA-256 linked Witness Chain. If it happened, we can prove it. Tamper-evident by design — not a log file, a hash-linked chain that breaks if anyone touches it.

SHA-256 · WORM
03 ——

⚖ Humans Govern, AI Advises

AI proposes actions. A rule engine decides. Humans retain final authority through structured oversight — per EU AI Act Article 14. No high-risk action executes without a person in the loop. Designed-in, not bolted-on.

EU AI Act Art. 14
04 ——

🚫 Fail-Fast, Not Fail-Silent

Constraint violations trigger HTTP 422 rejections. The system stops before damage occurs. Zero silent mutations. An agent that cannot be governed cannot act.
